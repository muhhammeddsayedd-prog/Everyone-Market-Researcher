## Recovery, retention and security limits

- The selected-account replay rewind and final restoration must compare the recorded owner, `updated_at` and snapshotted operational fields before updating. A newer legitimate update blocks replacement; local isolated fixture results are not a hosted compare-and-set receipt.
- No automatic paid POST retry, actor resurrection, synchronous actor replacement, source-login/cookie input, video download/transcription, AI call or new provider.

Delayed external delivery is distinct from unfinished database work and must be inventoried if not settled.

- Production pilot backfill, recurring schedules, external notifications, PR #13 merge and M05 remain separate decisions. This representative packet alone cannot satisfy the five-account/full-backfill/recurring or production acceptance criteria.
